ABOUT OUR CHURCH

More Than a Church, We Are a Family.

We are a Methodist Church family committed to sharing God's love, growing in faith and serving our community. We believe that the church is more than a building. It is a family where people come together to worship, encourage one another, discover God's purpose and make a positive difference in the world around them.
